My Story.

I'm Hafiz, a web developer from Ishwardi, Pabna, Bangladesh, who loves building user-focused, performance-driven web applications with both front-end and back-end technologies.

My journey began in 2024, when I taught myself C programming through YouTube tutorials. Inspired by Jhankar Mahbub Sir's videos, I then stepped into web development with the Programming Hero course.

Balancing the course with a part-time job wasn't easy, but I kept learning and building so I never lost the flow.

In 2026, I completed the course, earned my certificate and built full-stack projects like CureMedix, Elkano64 and NewsNexus. Right now I'm actively looking for opportunities where I can solve real-world problems through web development.

Outside of coding, I enjoy traveling, video games, self-improvement books, working out, and playing cricket or badminton. I'm a huge fan of Cristiano Ronaldo and I believe in a never-give-up mentality. My goal is to become an expert developer and contribute to cutting-edge projects that impact millions worldwide. And you know what? I love what I do! 💜
